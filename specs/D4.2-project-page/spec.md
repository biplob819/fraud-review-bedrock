# D4.2 — Project page and supporting artifacts

**Wave 5. Depends on:** D4.1, D4.3, B3.2, B3.3, B3.4, B3.5.

## Goal
One page that tells the story of the build and links every supporting document, with no video (D-45).

## Outputs
- `docs/project.md`: what it is, one alert walked through in screenshots, the evaluation results, what real use would need, and links.
- `docs/prd-onepager.md`: a one-page PRD that says what changed from the full plan.
- The PRD, decision log, data card and build notes are linked.

## Rules
- Both pages are published files under the honesty gate; numbers match the evaluation summary.
- The page says plainly that there is no video and that the hosted demo is planned for v0.2.
