# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a place where people can ask questions, share work and disagree without fear. This code is modelled on the Contributor Covenant, written here in our own words.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Assume the other person is trying to help.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Say what you mean plainly. Do not mock a question, even a simple one.
- Accept that people have different levels of experience and different first languages.
- Own your mistakes and fix them.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, threats or insults of any kind.
- Slurs, or demeaning comments about someone's background, identity or body.
- Unwelcome sexual attention.
- Sharing someone's private information without their consent.
- Repeated disruption of an issue, discussion or review after a request to stop.

## Scope

This applies in the issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space that is part of this project. It also applies when someone speaks for the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who break this code. We will try to give a warning first, but we may act at once when someone is at risk.

To report a problem, use the private "Report a vulnerability" form on the repository's Security tab and say that it is a conduct report. Only the maintainers can see it. We will treat what you tell us as private and will not name you to the person you report.
